Using video URLs

Paste a direct link to a playable video file above, usually ending in .mp4 or .webm. Social-media share/watch links usually cannot be loaded directly; use a permitted video file URL or import a video you have permission to use.

TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and other social links usually open a webpage rather than a raw video file, and may require login or block cross-origin access. ClipIt cannot bypass those restrictions or DRM. For those sources, use the platform’s permitted download/export option and import the resulting file. Only edit videos you have permission to use.
